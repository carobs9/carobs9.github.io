> This is an academic project completed for a the Natural Language Processing (NLP) master’s level class at DIKU (University of Copenhagen, Faculty of Computer Science). Please note that the project was completed along with two other students.

> It displays a compilation of NLP related tasks. Mainly, the paper goes over a survey of techniques to create a question-answering tool for different languages (Arabic, Bengali and Indonesian). 

> You can check out the final paper [here](/files/NLP_Final_Report.pdf).
